The space

A room built
around green.

Zen Zone is a new, all-vegetarian café on the Lavale road — the kind of place that opens at eleven and lets the afternoon run long. Inside, the ceiling is teak slat and the light is low. Outside, the deck looks out at the hills.

Photographs from the café.

  1. 01

    The green ceiling

    Trailing ferns and yellow wisteria hang from the beams above the entrance. Brushed past on the way in, they set the pace of the room.

  2. 02

    Wood, marble, olive

    Warm timber tables on a cool marble floor. A long olive counter with three white slats — the same green as the sign outside.

  3. 03

    The hill side

    The front rolls open to a terrace above the road. Late afternoon, the trees go amber and the fans keep turning.
